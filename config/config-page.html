Display

What to look for

How far to search and how far ahead. Values outside the allowed range are clamped when saving.

Sort order

Every line on the watch gets a score = Σ scale × value and the highest score is shown first. Stop scales are weights per meter of distance to the stop; route scales are weights per second until the bus. So a negative scale means nearer / sooner first, and a bigger negative number makes that distance or wait matter more. 0 ignores it; a positive scale prefers farther / later.

Bus routes

Routes you care about, by provider route id (Metro Transit: the route number, e.g. 2 or 901 for the Blue Line; OneBusAway: e.g. 1_100225). The name is what the watch shows. Scale is per second of waiting: −2 ranks this route's buses ahead of a −1 route arriving at the same time. Color is how the route is drawn on the watch; Auto uses the transit agency's color (or a default).

No routes yet; every route uses the default route scale.

Pinned stops

Places you ride to: a route and the stop you get off at (e.g. Home, Work). When you're on that route the watch shows the arrival there. Scale is per meter, like stop scales; empty uses the default pinned-stop scale. Choose stop picks it from the route's stop list.

Trips

Places you need to arrive by a time (e.g. Work by 9:00 on weekdays). The phone plans a trip with your routes and the watch reminds you when to leave. No day selected = every day. Choose stop uses a stop's position as the destination.

Stop scales

Per-stop weights per meter of walking. Use a smaller negative number (e.g. −0.5) for a stop you don't mind walking farther to, a bigger one (e.g. −3) for one you only use when it's right there.

No per-stop scales; all stops use the default stop scale.

Learning

Each time you press SELECT on a line the watch records what you picked out of what was shown. From these picks the app learns weights that override the scales above, so the lines you tend to pick move to the top.
